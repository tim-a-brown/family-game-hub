Licence:  CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), same as the code that makes them.

Why synthesised: the plan was to use real recorded CC0 loops (OpenGameArt CC0 tracks, Kenney audio),
but the build machine could not reach OpenGameArt, Kenney, Freesound, the Internet Archive or
Incompetech (network policy). Rather than use anything with an unclear licence, the beds are
generated by make-music.py with physically modelled instruments (Karplus-Strong plucked strings,
modal marimba and music box, breathy flute), detuned supersaw and noise pads, chorus, soft
saturation and a generated convolution reverb. To swap in recorded music later, drop a seamless
loop named <table>.mp3 here and update its length in loops.json and in games/pinball/tables/<table>.js (music.samples).

Format: 32 kHz mono MP3, 64 kbps, seamless loops (lengths in loops.json, in samples at 32 kHz).
