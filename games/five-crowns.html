📖 How to play Five Crowns

Goal: Lowest score after 11 rounds wins.

Rounds: 11 rounds — players are dealt 3 cards in round 1, increasing by 1 each round up to 13 cards in round 11.

Wild cards: The rank matching the round number is wild (3s in round 1, 4s in round 2 … Kings in round 11). Jokers are always wild.

Going out: On your turn, if you can arrange all cards into valid books (3+ same rank) or runs (3+ consecutive same suit), lay down and go out. Everyone else gets one final turn.

Scoring: Count the value of unmatched cards left in hand: 3–10 = face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13, Wild=20, Joker=50. Enter 0 for the player who went out.
