📖 How to play Rook

Goal: First team to reach the target score (default 300) wins.

Points in the deck: 5s=5pts each, 10s=10pts each, 14s=10pts each, Rook card=20pts. Total: 120 pts per hand.

Bidding: Players bid how many of the 120 points they think their team can capture. Minimum bid is typically 70. The highest bidder names the trump suit and takes the nest.

Scoring: Bidding team scores their captured points if they meet or exceed their bid; if they fall short, they lose their bid amount (go set). The opposing team always scores their captured points. Record each team's points captured this hand, plus the bid and whether the bidding team made it.

Rook card: Highest trump, worth 20 points.
