▶ How to Play Lorcana

🎯 Goal

Be the first player to collect 20 lore. You gain lore by sending your characters on quests.

🔁 Turn Structure

1
Ready — unexert (straighten) all your cards at the start of your turn.
2
Set — draw a card, then optionally put one card from your hand into your inkwell (it must have the ink symbol). Tap an ink dot below to track.
3
Draw — play characters, actions, items, and songs by paying their ink cost. Tap ink dots to show how many are spent.
4
Actions — Quest with characters (they exert; you gain lore equal to their lore value), or Challenge opposing characters. New characters can't act on their first turn (summoning sickness).

✨ Ink Tracker (dots)

The 7 dots beneath each player represent their inkwell. Fill in dots as you add ink cards throughout the game — your total dots = ink available each turn. Tap a dot to toggle it used/ready.

🃏 Key Terms

⭐
Lore — victory points. Track with the +1 / +3 buttons.
💧
Ink — your resource to play cards. Grows by one each turn you ink a card (max 7 in starter rules).
⚔️
Challenger — a keyword that gives bonus strength when challenging.
🛡️
Bodyguard — must be challenged before other characters.
🏃
Rush — this character can challenge on the turn it's played.
🎵
Shift — play on top of a matching character for a discount.

🏆 Winning

The first player to reach 20 lore wins immediately — even during your opponent's turn. This counter highlights the winner automatically.
