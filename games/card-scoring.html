📖 How to Play Spades

Goal: First team to reach the point target wins (default 500).

Bidding: Each team bids how many tricks (out of 13) they'll take. Spades are always trump.

Scoring:

  • Make your bid: bid × 10 points
  • Miss your bid: −(bid × 10) points
  • Tricks over your bid: +1 each (but accumulate as bags)
  • Nil bid: Partner plays alone. +100 if no tricks taken, −100 if any tricks taken.

Bags: Overtricks (bags) score +1 each but every 10 bags = −100 penalty (optional rule — toggle below).

📖 How to Play Hearts

Goal: Have the lowest score when any player reaches 100 points.

Scoring per round:

  • Each ♥ heart card taken: +1 point
  • Queen of Spades ♛: +13 points
  • Avoid hearts and the Queen of Spades — they hurt!

Shooting the Moon 🌕: If one player takes all 13 hearts AND the Queen of Spades, they score 0 and every other player gets +26. Use the toggle when entering a round where someone pulls this off.

Passing: Before each round, players pass 3 cards to an opponent (direction rotates each hand).

📖 How to Play Gin Rummy

Goal: First player to reach the target score (default 500) wins.

Hands: Each player gets 10 cards. Draw from the deck or discard pile; discard one card per turn. Build sets (3+ of a kind) and runs (3+ consecutive same suit).

Going out:

  • Knock: When your unmatched cards (deadwood) total 10 or less, lay down your melds and knock. The opponent lays off cards if possible. Knocker scores the difference in deadwood values.
  • Gin: If you have zero deadwood, call Gin for a 25-point bonus on top of opponent's deadwood.
  • Undercut: If your opponent knocks but you have equal or less deadwood, you win and score the difference +25.

Card values: Aces = 1, face cards = 10, others = face value. Enter each round as the points scored by the winner of that hand.
