Craps rules & tips

Come-out roll (puck OFF)

Pass Line: Win on 7 or 11, lose on 2/3/12 (craps). Any other number sets the point.
Don't Pass: Opposite of Pass — win on 2 or 3, push on 12, lose on 7/11.

Point phase (puck ON)

The shooter must roll the point again before a 7 to win the Pass Line. A 7 now means seven-out (Pass loses, Don't Pass wins).

Come / Don't Come work like Pass / Don't Pass but are placed after the point is set. When a Come bet's first roll is a number, the bet moves onto that number (shown as a gold "C" tag) and wins if the number repeats before a 7. Don't Come moves the same way (black "DC" tag) and wins on a 7.

Place bets

Bet on 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 10 to roll before a 7. Pays 9:5 on 4/10, 7:5 on 5/9, and 7:6 on 6/8.

Field & propositions

Field: One-roll bet — wins on 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, 12 (2 and 12 pay 2:1). Loses on 5, 6, 7, 8.
Any 7: One roll, pays 4:1. Hard 8: 4+4 before a 7 or an easy 8, pays 9:1.

Tips

The Pass Line has one of the lowest house edges in the casino. Place bets on 6 and 8 are also player-friendly. Proposition bets (Any 7, Hardways) carry the highest house edge — play them for fun.
