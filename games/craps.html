📋 Craps Rules & Tips ▼

Come-Out Roll (Puck = OFF)

Pass Line: Win on 7 or 11, lose on 2/3/12 (craps). Any other number sets the point.
Don't Pass: Opposite of Pass — win on 2 or 3, push on 12, lose on 7/11.

Point Phase (Puck = ON)

The shooter must roll the point number before rolling a 7 to win the Pass Line. A 7 now means seven-out (Pass loses, Don't Pass wins).
Come / Don't Come work like Pass / Don't Pass but are placed after the point is set.

Place Bets

You can bet any of the place numbers (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) to hit before a 7. Pays 9:5 on 4/10, 7:5 on 5/9, and 7:6 on 6/8.

Field & Proposition

Field: One-roll bet — wins on 2,3,4,9,10,11,12 (2 and 12 pay 2:1). Loses on 5,6,7,8.
Any 7: One-roll bet that the next roll is 7, pays 4:1 (high house edge — use sparingly).

💡 Tips

The Pass Line with full odds has the lowest house edge (~0.4%). Place bets on 6 and 8 are also player-friendly. Avoid proposition bets (Any 7, Hardways) except for fun — they carry the highest house edge.
