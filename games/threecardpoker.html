Ante: Your 3 cards play against the dealer's. Beat the dealer to win 1:1 plus any ante bonus. If the dealer doesn't qualify (Queen high or better), your ante wins 1:1.

Pair Plus: Pays on a pair or better no matter what the dealer has.

Hand ranks (high to low): Straight Flush, Three of a Kind, Straight, Flush, Pair, High Card.
