How to play

Goal: Bet on whether the Player hand, Banker hand, or a Tie will have a score closest to 9.

Card values: Aces = 1, 2–9 = face value, 10/J/Q/K = 0. Only the last digit of the total counts (e.g. 15 = 5).

Natural: If either hand totals 8 or 9 on the first two cards, no more cards are drawn.

Third card: Player draws if total ≤ 5. Banker follows a strict drawing table based on Banker total and Player's third card.

Payouts: Player wins pays 1:1 · Banker wins pays 1:1 minus 5% commission · Tie pays 8:1 (Banker bets push on a tie).
