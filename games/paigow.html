How to play

Goal: Split your 7 cards into a 5-card High hand and a 2-card Low hand. Both must beat the dealer's matching hands.

Setting: Tap 2 cards to move them to your Low hand (tap again to move back). Your High hand must outrank your Low hand. Auto-Set picks a split for you.

Joker: Plays as an Ace.

Payouts: Win both hands = 1:1 minus 5% commission · Lose both = lose bet · Win one = push.
