📖 How to Play Wizard

Goal: Highest score after all rounds wins.

Rounds: Deal 1 card in round 1, increasing by 1 per round. Number of rounds = 60 ÷ players (e.g. 15 rounds with 4 players). After dealing, flip the next card to set trump (Wizard = player picks, Jester = no trump).

Bidding: Each player bids exactly how many tricks they'll win. All bids are revealed simultaneously.

Wizard cards: Always win the trick (highest Wizard played leads). Jester cards: Always lose.

Scoring:

  • Exact bid: 20 + 10×bid points
  • Wrong bid: −10×|bid − tricks| points (penalty per trick off)
