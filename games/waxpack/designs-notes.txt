Wax Pack card designs (designs.js): how sure each one is, 2026-11-28

Every design is drawn in boxes from what is widely known of each set's look; no card scan was copied and no artwork
from a real card is used (the player pictures are the game's own paintings). The pages that would have shown each
year's cards side by side (card databases, Wikipedia's set articles) could not be reached from the session that drew
them, so the layouts come from what is commonly pictured of each set. "a" means the set's look as widely pictured
(border, picture window, where the name and team sit); "b" the main parts are right and the details are a guess;
"c" a fair guess at the year's look, to be checked against a real card. A horizontal card is drawn upright, since
the game's cards stand upright.

Topps (the flagship set of each year)
  a: 1953, 1957-1965, 1967-1968, 1970-1990 (1962 and 1987 wood grain, 1968 burlap, 1971 black borders, 1972 arched
     frame and bright team name, 1975 and 1990 two-color borders, 1982 hockey sticks, 1986 black band)
  b: 1952, 1954-1956, 1960 (horizontal in real life), 1969, 1974, 1991 (the fortieth year), 2001 (green borders),
     2007 (black borders)
  c: 1951 (the Red Backs and Blue Backs, the play read off the corner left out), 1966, 1992-2000, 2002-2006,
     2008-2025 (one look for 2008-2012 and one full-bleed look for 2013-2025)
Bowman (Topps): a 1948, 1953, 1955 (the television set); b 1949-1952, 1954; c 1989-2025 (one look)
Play Ball (Topps): b 1939-1941
Upper Deck: b 1989-1990; c 1991-2010 (two looks)
Fleer: b 1981-1992 (1987 blue, 1988 stripes, 1989 pinstripes, 1991 yellow, 1992 green); c 1963, 1993-2007
O-Pee-Chee: Topps' design of the same year with O-Pee-Chee's name and French and English headings on the back,
  1965-1994 (one grade below Topps' own for that year)
Ultra c 1991-2007, Flair c 1993-1998, SP c 1993-2010, Collector's Choice c 1994-1998, SkyBox c 1997-2001
Goudey: a 1933, b 1934, c 1935-1941 (the 1935 four-in-one and 1938 Heads-Up drawn bodies are left out)
Public domain: Old Judge a 1887-1890, T206 a 1909-1911, T205 b 1911, T207 b 1912, Cracker Jack b 1914-1915,
  The Sporting News M101 b 1916-1917, American Caramel c 1909-1922 (one look for its several sets)

Backs: the cardboard color and inks are a guess for most years (c), except the gray stock of the 1950s to 1980s
Topps and the white stock of the 1990s on. Topps' cartoon panels are not drawn; a framed panel holds a line from
the player's own record instead.

Years with no design here use the game's own six designs (cardface.js): 1918-1932, 1942-1947, and any year a card's
maker made no set.
