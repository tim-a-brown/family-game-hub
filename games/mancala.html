How to play Mancala

Goal: Capture more seeds than your opponent. When all of one side's pits are empty, the game ends and the most seeds in a store wins.

Your turn: Pick up all seeds from one of your pits and drop one in each pit going counter-clockwise, including your store — but skip your opponent's store.

Extra turn: If your last seed lands in your own store, go again!

Capture: If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it plus all seeds in the pit directly opposite.

Game end: When one side is empty, the other player collects all remaining seeds on their side.
