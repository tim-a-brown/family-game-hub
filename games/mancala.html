📖 How to Play Mancala ▼

Goal: Capture more seeds than your opponent. The player with the most seeds in their store when all of one side's pits are empty wins.

Setup: 6 pits per player, 4 seeds each. Your store is on your right.

Your turn: Pick up all seeds from one of your pits. Drop one seed in each pit going counter-clockwise, including your store — but skip your opponent's store.

Extra turn: If your last seed lands in your own store, you get another turn! 🎉

Capture: If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture that seed plus all seeds in the pit directly opposite — they all go into your store.

Game end: When all 6 pits on one player's side are empty, the other player captures all remaining seeds on their side.
