How to play

Goal: Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over.

Values: Number cards count face value, J/Q/K count 10, Aces count 1 or 11.

Actions: Hit (take a card), Stand, Double Down (double the bet, take exactly one card), Split (two equal cards become two hands).

Dealer: Draws to 17. Blackjack pays 3:2, wins pay 1:1, ties push.
