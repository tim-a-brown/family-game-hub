The Timbuktu Manuscripts' power: "You can hold 10 cards in your hand" (2026-11-13)
=================================================================================

Tim: "any relics exist that allow a player to hold more cards in their hand? there are a lot of draw opportunities, but
having to keep the hand at 8 or less is a consistent constraint", then "yes - that's fine".

The Timbuktu Manuscripts and the Quipu leave the Printing Press's power (draw one more card each turn) for a power of
their own: "You can hold 10 cards in your hand." The Manuscripts are the card the power is named for (no fx), the Quipu
has fx 'manuscripts'. Both still cost 2. The Printing Press's power keeps nine Relics.

The engine and the page
-----------------------
  engine.js  handMax(S, o): a player's own limit, the rules' handMax (8), or HOLD (10) with the power in play (cfg.hold
             tries another number in test games). endTurn reads it. Exported as E.handMax.
  ui.js      the discard screen (endTurn, discardOne) and the caption "Hand limit 10: discard 1" read E.handMax; the
             reader says under such a Relic "Without the Quipu, you can hold 8 cards when your turn ends."; RULES:
             "You can hold 8 cards when your turn ends, or 10 with the Timbuktu Manuscripts or the Quipu in play."
  Robots     the Relic worth table RW (relicWorth, shared by evalS and evalM): the power counts 0.2 a round still to come,
             as any Relic with no table entry did. Counting it from the hand was rejected (a worth that grew with the hand
             pays the robot for keeping cards it could play); a higher worth (0.4) made the robots play it twice as often
             and win no more (below).
  Lessons    none names the Manuscripts or the Quipu. "Your turn" says "You can hold 8 cards when your turn ends", which
             is true on its table (no such Relic); the Relics lesson plays the Compass and the Printing Press, unchanged.
  Legacy     the Manuscripts' line runs from Timbuktu (a Domain); the Quipu has none; no Relic's line runs from either.
             check-set.js: no Legacy Line joins two Relics with one power.
  Tests      deeds-test.js: 13 more (the words, the power, the cost, 10 for its player and 8 for the other, 11 keep 10,
             10 kept with the Quipu, the Quipu not beside the Manuscripts, the Manuscripts beside the Printing Press,
             cfg.hold, a copy of the table, robots in whole games). check-set.js nothing wrong; powers-test.js 96 passed;
             deeds-test.js 149 passed; players-test.js 55 passed.

How full hands get (robot games, Normal against Normal, 100 games, before the change): 26% of turns end over 8 cards,
and 6.3 cards a game are discarded to the limit (both players). With 10, 2.3% of turns would still end over it.

Hold 10 sits among the Relics (48.6 to 55.0), 0.6 under the Printing Press it replaces. 9 measures no better than 10, so
10 stays. To the robots the power is worth about what the Abacus and the Boundary Stone are: little. They discard the
dearest cards when over the limit and never plan a hand, so a player who chooses what to keep should get more from it.

a Relic's line rests on about 500 games and wanders by 2 to 3 points: the nine Relics with the Printing Press's
power in this very run go from 48.7 to 55.0):
  When the card is in the deck: the Relics the game had before 50.5% (44.8 to 55.6)
    Timbuktu Manuscripts 47.1% (503 games), Quipu 45.8% (485 games): -3.4 and -4.7 from the Relics, inside their range
    (Rain Gauge 44.8, Sextant 45.6). newcards.js lists neither outside what the Relics had.
  When the card was played: Timbuktu Manuscripts 40.6% (106 games), Quipu 42.2% (90 games), the lowest Relics; the
    robots play it in a fifth of the games they have it, mostly late, when a full hand is already a sign of trouble.
  Elsewhere in the same run: Mysore Rockets 55.6% and Birchbark Canoe 55.5% top the Relics; Rain Gauge 44.8% is the
    lowest. Damascus Steel's power is the strongest in relicfx.js too (55.0%): the relics proposal flags it.
