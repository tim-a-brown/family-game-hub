The Relic powers of 2026-11-13, built (relics-proposal.md, with Tim's decisions)
================================================================================

Tim: "far sight seems like a lot -- maybe they just reveal the first of the two cards they draw each turn while in
play? and stores - maybe limit it to being active only while not leading in renown? the other decisions are fine".

What was built (the card sentence, the cost; the key of the power is the card it is named for, fx on the others)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Foresight     Antikythera Mechanism (3 -> 2), Dresden Codex, 2: "At the start of your turn, before you draw, look at the
                top 3 cards of your draw pile and put them back in any order."  (key codex)
  Landing       Viking Longship, Voyaging Canoe (3 -> 2): "You can play your Figures at the Crossroads."
  Convoy        The Wheel, 1: "When one of your Figures walks, one other Figure of yours at the same Domain can walk
                with it."
  Railway       Steam Locomotive, 1: "Once a turn, one of your Figures at your Gate can walk to any Domain where you have
                another Figure."
  Charge        Stirrup, 1: "Your Figures can start a clash on a turn they walk."
  Rebuilding    Inca Rope Bridge (1 -> 2): "Once a game, when a round ends, mend one of your broken Seals."
  Homecoming    Cyrus Cylinder, 1: "Once a round, when one of your Figures is knocked out, it goes back into your hand."
                At Rapa Nui, which would put it on top of the draw pile, its owner chooses (the robots: the hand).
  Bastion       Greek Fire, 2: "Your Figures have +1 Might when defending at your Gate or your Outpost."
  Volley        Mysore Rockets, 2: "Once a turn, one of your Figures can start a clash with an enemy Figure at the next
                Domain, without moving. If your Figure loses, nothing happens to it." The clash is fought at the
                target's Domain (its rules, its home region); the defender who wins still has what winning there gives.
  Decipher      Rosetta Stone, 2: "Each Legacy Line you make scores 1 more Renown."
  Far sight     Telescope (2 -> 1): "Each opponent shows you the first card they draw each turn." (Tim's change: it was
                "your opponents play with their hands face up"; then "the drawn card is shown briefly, then held
                normally": nothing stays face up, nothing is kept in the state, the turn log names the card.)
  Reading       Braille, 2: "When you play a Scholar Figure, you draw one more card."
  Stores        Clay Tablet, 2: "While you are not ahead in Renown, you gain 1 more Spark at the start of each turn."
                (Tim's change. Ahead: more Renown than every other side; a team counts its side's Renown.)
  Library Cave  Dunhuang Star Chart, 2: "Once a turn, you can pay 1 Spark to take a card from your discard pile into your
                hand."
  Safe conduct  Paiza (3 -> 1): "Your Figures never miss a turn."
  Kept: Compass (Compass, Astrolabe, Birchbark Canoe, Stick Chart, 1), the jump (key balloon now: Montgolfier Balloon,
  Wright Flyer, Sextant, Harrison's Watch, 3), Printing Press (Press, Paper, Telegraph, 2), Manuscripts (2), Abacus
  (Abacus, Rain Gauge, 2), Boundary Stone (1), Damascus Steel (alone now, 2 -> 3).

A saved game keeps the powers it began with: cfg.rfx 2 (STD, G.rfx on the page); without it every Relic reads its
power from RFX1 (engine.js), what it was before, so a saved game's Antikythera Mechanism still jumps. Costs are today's.

Leonardo, who had four of the changing Relics (the Antikythera Mechanism, Viking Longship and Voyaging Canoe left the
jump, the Telescope the Printing Press), is 2.8 lower on both runs: the jump measured 51.9, Foresight 48.6 and Landing
50.4. Sor Juana stays the weakest: on her own pairs (sets.js ... sorjuana) she is 50.3 before and 45.2 after; with
the Clay Tablet's old power for everyone 46.3, with the Inca Rope Bridge back to the Compass at 1 as well 50.3. So her
loss is the Tablet (in every deck: it helps the side behind, which her opponents often are not) and the Rope Bridge,
which costs her a second Spark for a power her brawler robot seldom gets to use. Sun Tzu is up on 91000 only (on his
own pairs 50.9 before, 51.8 after; with the Stirrup back to the Compass 52.0: the Stirrup is not why).
Suggestions, not made: the Inca Rope Bridge to another set (or a Relic with a power that suits her robots in its
place in Sor Juana's); Leonardo, whose Guide makes Relics cheaper, could take a jump Relic back into his set.
