What these say. A pack always holds as many copies a player is missing as its size, so the whole set takes the same
time in any order: 406 copies after the starter deck and the welcome packs, three a day. The order decides only WHEN
the ready decks fill. Mixed, a new player waits four months to own a second ready deck, where it was one. In series
the first month is exactly what it was (30.0 days, as before the third printing), and the 150 are what comes after.
A larger welcome buys nine days and makes the first evening 60 cards to turn over.

(89.5 days), where packs of 3 make it more than four. Both are one line each near the top of coll.js. Try others with
  node scripts/anoria/pacing.js 200 3 0.5 <daily> <one-time> 0 <welcome, as 12,12,12> <series|mixed> <second-series pack>

What these say. Domains ride on the packs a player already earns (one a day at most, and three once each), so how fast
they come is one number: the chance that an earned pack holds one. At a half, a player has 21 Domains to choose 15
from on the first evening, 36 after a month, and the last one at about four months, a little before the last card
(7% of players find their last Domain after their last card). In every pack, all 75 are found in two months, which
is about when a keen player has two ready decks: a good fit if choosing a stack is meant to matter early. One in three
drags past the cards. The eleven Domain cards add 11 cards to the set and about 8 days in all. In the first series
they turn up from the first welcome pack, and the first series takes 38.7 days where it took 30; in the second series
the first month is exactly what it was, and nobody meets a card that changes a Domain until it is over.
Recommended (Tim's to decide): Domains as a place of their own in a pack (DOMAINS_IN_PACKS 'slot'), two in each
welcome pack, and one in an earned pack half the time (DOMAIN_SLOT 0.5), which coll.js has; and the Domain cards in
the first series (DOMAIN_CARD_SERIES 1), which it also has, because cards that change Domains are part of what makes
Domains worth choosing. Packs of their own for Domains were not built: they would need a new way to earn them and a
screen, and the slot does the same job with one number. Each is one line near the top of coll.js. Try others with
  node scripts/anoria/pacing.js 150 3 0.5 0 0 0 0 0 - <chance of a Domain> <Domains in a welcome pack> <series of the Domain cards>
