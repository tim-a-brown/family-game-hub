The figure is how many points of win rate a ready deck gains or loses when it plays under this Guide in place of its own.
"none" is a deck with no Guide at all, so the first five are worth about 1.5 points each.

The powers, as a player reads them
  Joan of Arc     While the other player has more Renown than you, your Figures have +1 Might in every clash they start.
  Saladin         The first time each turn that one of your Figures wins a clash it started, you gain 1 Spark.
  Shakespeare     The first time each round that one of your Figures is defeated at the Crossroads, you score 1 Renown.
  Empress Wu      If you hold more Places than the other player when a round ends, you have 1 more Spark on your next turn.
  Akbar           If you have Figures of two or more factions at the Crossroads when a round ends, you score 1 Renown.
  Mansa Musa      Up to 2 Spark that you have not spent when your turn ends are yours to spend on your next turn.
  Sor Juana       Your first Deed each turn costs 1 less.
  Marie Curie     The third card you play each turn costs 1 less. (unchanged, measured again beside them)

What was learned on the way
- Cards are not what is scarce. A player draws two a turn and can pay for one or two, so a hand after drawing is 7 cards or more
  nearly always (in 500 games it was 4 or fewer on about one turn in a hundred for Pathfinders and never for the other decks).
  Every power that pays in cards reads as no Guide at all, and the second sentence of Franklin's power ("If your hand is 4 cards
  or fewer after you draw, draw one more") changed no result in 3,000 games: Franklin reads +1.2, +0.9, +0.5 and Sor Juana, with
  his first sentence alone, +1.1, +0.8, +0.6. Spark, Might and Renown are what a power can pay in.
- A point of Might that is always on is worth far more than it looks: +10 to +20. It has to be tied to one kind of moment
  (a clash you start, while behind) to come down to where the others are.
- The robots were taught one thing: what Akbar's court is worth while it stands (COURT_W in engine.js, 0.6 of a Renown). They were
  not taught to save Spark for Mansa Musa or to hold Places for Empress Wu: the first made them worse, and they already hold
  Places. A person who saves on purpose will get more from Mansa Musa than the robots show.
- While these were measured the seven were still cards in ready decks (Joan of Arc and Saladin in Legion; Mansa Musa, Shakespeare
  and Sor Juana in Court). No power reads the Guide's own card, but Mansa Musa the card brings 2 Spark when he arrives, which
  leaves Court more Spark to keep under Mansa Musa the Guide: his Court figures (+4.5, -0.8, +0.3, +5.0) may run a little high.
