WHAT CHANGED
  George Washington   5/5 to 5/4. At 5 Might he won 58.5% of the 837 games he was played in, 3 points above any Valor card
                      the game had (55.4%), and 53.4% of games with him in the deck (Valor 49.7 to 52.3). At 4 Might, on
                      another set of games: 54.5% when played and 53.1% in the deck, inside what Valor had (to 58.3 and 53.7).
  Menelik II          left at 4/3 (the draft had already taken him from 4/4). He is in the deck of an ordinary share of
                      winners (+0.6 both times), but won 57.1% and 61.0% of the few games he was played in (343 and 406).
                      Measured on purpose (onecard.js, below: 819 games played) it is 56.0%, inside what Valor had. He is
                      played when an African Place is on the table, and one of those is a Crossroads, the Library.
  Johannes Gutenberg  left at 3/3. The draft's games could not play Relics, so his skill (draw a card when you play a Relic)
                      did nothing there. With Relics played he is 47.1% and 44.6% in the deck beside Discovery's 46.3% and 45.1%.
  Nothing else is outside what its faction had. Relics and Deeds with one effect are the same card to the engine, so the
  differences between them below (Braille +3.0, Clay Tablet -4.3, both the Printing Press's power) are the measure wandering.

TWO RELICS WITH ONE POWER
  The rule: you can have 2 Relics in play at a time, and they have to be Relics with different powers. So nothing adds up.
  The other way (both are played and the power counts twice) was played against it. It makes no difference these robots can
  measure, a point either way in 500 games, so there was no balance reason to choose, and the rule is the one that is a
  single sentence and is what the game already did with a second copy of one Relic.

  node scripts/anoria/relics2.js 500
