Seven new cards and two new Quick powers (2026-11-12)
=====================================================

Tim approved seven cards, found only in packs (the third printing, pr 3, in no ready deck), and a Quick power of its
own for Testudo and for the Defence of Syracuse, which had shared Phalanx's (Phalanx itself is unchanged):

  The Roman Triumph        Deed, Valor       a Domain where you have a Figure scores you double at this round's count
  Medici Patronage         Deed, Arts        every Culture Domain you hold scores double at this round's count
  The Hanseatic League     Deed, Statecraft  every Commerce Domain you hold scores double (Medici's effect, dk Commerce)
  The Jubilee of 1300      Deed, Wonder      every Domain you hold scores double at this round's count
  The Tribune's Veto       Quick Deed, Statecraft   cancels a Deed the other player plays that would score Renown or
                           move, stun or knock out one of your Figures; that Deed goes to their discard pile
  Pope Leo I               Figure, Statecraft, Ward: the other player's Deeds have no effect on your Figures where he
                           stands, and the Domain's power there works for them only where it helps them
  The Theodosian Walls     Deed, Valor       a Seal while you have fewer than 4 (a broken one mended, or a fourth)
  Testudo (new power)      Quick: a Figure of yours that loses a clash it started on the other player's side falls
                           back to your Gate instead of being knocked out
  The Defence of Syracuse  Quick: an enemy Figure that would win a clash it started at your Gate or Outpost is driven
  (new power)              back to its own Gate, and nobody is knocked out

The engine: S.dbl (the doubled counts, emptied at the count, a Domain doubled once however many Deeds double it),
LASTING (the four lie on the table in S.dip until the count), wardAt, vetoable, handFx, the two reactions at the end of
clash(), WALL_MAX and P.sm. scripts/anoria/deeds-test.js checks every card against its words (66 positions).

How they were measured
----------------------
  node scripts/anoria/run.js cards 6000            random decks of two factions, every card; newcards.js beside the
                                                   older cards of each faction (cardsvar.js in the session scratchpad
                                                   ran the same games with other numbers on a card)
  node scripts/anoria/twocopies.js <card> 60       each ready deck with none, one and two copies of a card
  node scripts/anoria/newdecks.js                  built decks against the ready decks
  node scripts/anoria/endings.js "after" '{}' 200 91000   the ready decks: must come out exactly as before
  node scripts/anoria/rarity.js 6000 3             the tiers, of which only the seven's were taken

A card's win rate over about 1,700 games wanders by about 1.2 points from one set of games to another (the same
numbers with seed 202 in place of 101 moved single cards by up to 3 points: F and F2 below).
