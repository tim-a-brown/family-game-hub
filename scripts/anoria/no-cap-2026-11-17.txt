Figures at a Domain: no limit, and the limits the setup offers (2026-11-17)

Tim, 2026-11-17: "is there a good reason for the three card limit at a domain in arvela?" There was none: at most three
of one player's Figures at a Domain was in the first version's rules, and no balance run had tested it. Tim's decision:
remove the limit. The same day: "give game options to cap domains at 4-6 cards apiece", so the setup offers a limit of
4, 5 or 6 for one game (No limit is the default). Thermopylae keeps its own limit of two. A game saved under the old
limit of three finishes under it. In the engine the limit is cfg.cap: 0 for none (STD), 3 the old rule (DEF, E.OLD_CAP).

Reading it: the length of a game, the knock-outs and the share of games won on Seals do not move with the limit. The
spread between the five ready decks is 10 to 11 points under the old limit and under none, and 12 to 14 in between: the
Legion deck (43%) is the one that loses by it at every limit above three, and the Court deck gains at 4 and 5. Legacy
lines go from 2.4 a game to 2.9 with no limit, because more of a player's Figures can stand together. Nobody plays the
ready decks any more (they are for these scripts, the lessons and games saved with them).

Reading it: with each deck played by the robot that played it best under the old limit (STY in sets.js), the decks
are 4.5 points apart at 3, 5.3 at 4, 9.4 at 5, 8.9 at 6 and 11.7 with no limit. Sun Tzu, Leonardo and Cleopatra gain
with no limit; Sor Juana (43.4%), Joan of Arc and Mansa Musa lose. With the plain Normal robot on every deck the spread
is 8.6 at 3 and 7.3 with no limit, so no wider. Part of the 11.7 is therefore the styles, which were found by trying
all four against the field under the old limit, and not the decks alone. Nothing was tuned for this change: the sets,
the styles and the robots' weights are as they were. A limit of 4 keeps the sets about where they were tuned.
The lines "the five ready decks of before: 9.6" are sets.js's own fixed footnote.
