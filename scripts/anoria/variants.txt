When it happens (two sets with the log on): the last card is drawn in round 8.6 on average, never before round 7,
and in 98% of cases in the game's last round or the one before. The player then draws 3 cards from the new pile on
average (13 at most). The new pile is their knocked-out Figures and the cards they discarded at the hand limit; Deeds
and Relics they played are not in it. Nobody, in any game, ended with no draw pile and no discard pile, so nobody
ever went without a card. A deck 10 cards bigger would have had its last card drawn in about 1 game in 1,000.
Tim's rule: 50 cards if a draw pile runs out in more than about one game in ten. It is on the line: 10.5% of games by
the widest count (someone draws their last card), 7.9% by the count that changes anything (someone needs a card
after it). The deck stays 40. If people draw harder than the robots do, Explorers and Legacy are where it will show.
