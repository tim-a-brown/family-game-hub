The first Figures that cost 1 Spark (Tim, 2026-11-13: "is there a case to make for a number of 1 might figures?", then
"i meant 1 spark more than 1 might actually but yes design and measure them"). Before them no Figure cost less than 2.
Seven people whose importance wasn't force, each with a small power true to them. All are 1 Spark and 1 Might, found
only in packs (cards.js P5). Sources in set-p5.json.

  Hunayn ibn Ishaq (Discovery, Baghdad, 860)   Scholar: draw a card when this Figure is played.
      The translator: he and his students put more than a hundred of Galen's books into Syriac and Arabic.
      Legacy Line from Hippocrates.
  Caroline Herschel (Discovery, Slough, 1787)  When you play this Figure, you gain 1 Spark.
      The astronomer: George III paid her fifty pounds a year, the first woman in Britain paid for work in science.
  Synesius of Cyrene (Statecraft, Cyrene, 400) No rules text: a Legacy Figure of Hypatia.
      The student: Hypatia's pupil, whose letters to her survive. (Yan Hui, Confucius's student, was the first choice,
      and is measured below; he left the same day under Tim's rule that every person has a contemporary record, since
      he is known only from the Analects, compiled after his death.)
  Paul Revere (Statecraft, Boston, 1775)       Swift: can walk on the turn it's played.
      The messenger: the express rider of Boston's patriot committees.
  Dominique-Jean Larrey (Valor, France, 1797)  Enemies can't clash with this Figure while a stronger Figure of yours is here.
      The surgeon: his flying ambulances went out under fire. The one power that needed engine work: the skill
      `shelter`, one condition in engine.js clashable (read through kOf, so the Walk to Canossa switches it off).
  Ban Zhao (Arts, Luoyang, 106)                Inspiring: your other Figures here get +1 Might.
      The teacher: she finished the history of the Han and taught the empress.
  Ibn Fadlan (Wonder, the Volga, 922)          Voyager: can walk to any Place.
      The traveler: secretary of the caliph's embassy to the Volga Bulgars.

Powers tried and not used: a Figure that counts as another person for Legacy Lines (engine work for little), and
1 Spark next turn (Herschel's 1 Spark now is a power the engine already had).

  The cheap bodies are played (about two more Figures a player) and clash and fall, but the Renown, how often a Domain
  is held unopposed, and the cards and Domains that reward outnumbering barely move. Thermopylae fills up more often,
  which is what it is for. Games run a fifth of a round shorter.

4. Decks that hold several (the same games): one deck with its 1-Spark Figures (4 to 8 of them) against the same deck
   without them, the same Guide and robot, both seats: 48.4% of 4000 games. Several cheap bodies make a deck a little
   weaker, not stronger: nothing to fear from a deck built around them.
