# Anoria art sources

Made with OpenAI's image model (gpt-image-2; marks with gpt-image-1.5 for transparency), using a streaming request.
`card-mock.html` lays the pieces out as cards.

## Card art style prompt

Graphic novel trading-card art in a screen-printed poster style: flat bold colour shapes, heavy black spot shadows, a limited palette of four or five inks, visible halftone dot texture and slightly offset print registration, rough paper grain, a strong silhouette and an angular, dynamic composition seen from a low angle, stylised expressive faces with real character, not pretty or idealised. No airbrushing, no soft glow, no glossy rendering, no photorealism, no smooth gradients. Portrait orientation, full bleed, no text, no letters, no numbers, no border, no frame.

Then: `Subject: Palette: <four inks>. <who, doing what, where>.`

## Marks (Spark, Might, Renown) style prompt

An abstract game icon made of a few bold hand-cut paper shapes, like a mid-century paper cut-out collage: simple organic geometry, only warm amber, black and cream, slightly rough scissor-cut edges and a faint paper texture, no realism, no outlines of objects, playful and full of character, reads instantly at a tiny size. Centered, filling most of the frame, on a fully transparent background. It includes a large plain cream shape in its middle where a big number will be added later. No text, no numbers, no letters, no star or sunburst shapes, no hexagons.

## Logo prompt

Logo design for a historical fantasy card game called ANORIA. Epic, gilded, hand-crafted look: real gold leaf and engraved brass with fine chased detail and deep relief, like a master engraver's emblem on the cover of an antique atlas. Rich warm gold with dark burnished shadows on a deep midnight-navy background with a very faint old-map texture. Perfectly centered, symmetrical, crisp, elegant, not cluttered. A tall, stylized capital letter A as the centerpiece. Its crossbar is replaced by a chronosphere: a small armillary sphere of three interlocking golden rings with a glowing core, orbiting through the middle of the A. Below it, the word ANORIA in wide-spaced engraved Roman capitals. No other text.

## Card back prompt

Card back design for a historical fantasy card game called ANORIA, matching its logo. Portrait playing card, full bleed. Deep midnight-navy field with a very faint antique map texture. In the center, a gilded chronosphere: an armillary sphere of three interlocking engraved gold rings with a small glowing golden core, set inside a tall oval astrolabe ring with four compass-star points at top, bottom, left and right. A slim ornate border of engraved gold filigree with small corner flourishes. Real gold leaf and chased brass look with deep relief and dark burnished shadows. Perfectly symmetrical top to bottom and left to right so it reads the same either way up. Elegant, restrained, not cluttered. No letters, no words, no numbers.
