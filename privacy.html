Privacy

Game Night, games.timbrown.xyz. Updated October 31, 2026.

Game Night is a family game site run by one family for its friends and relatives. There are no ads, no tracking for marketing, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What we keep

  • Your games: scores, high scores, game history, saved games, favorites and settings, so they follow you to any device you sign in on.
  • The names you type in for players, and any profile picture you choose or upload.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's id, name and email address, used only to sign you in and to show you which account you're using.
  • If you link with friends or family: your player name, picture, arcade initials and best scores, shown to the people you link with.

Where it's kept

In Google Firebase (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication). A PIN account can be opened by anyone who knows its PIN; a Google account can be opened only by that Google sign-in. Guest mode keeps everything on your device only.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask Google for anything beyond your basic profile and email.
  • We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or share it.

Removing your information

Email help.gamenight@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and everything stored with it.
